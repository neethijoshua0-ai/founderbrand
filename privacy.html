Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Founder Brand ("we", "us") provides LinkedIn ghostwriting and growth services to founders worldwide. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

Information you give us: when you fill in a form or book a call, we collect your name, email, company details, LinkedIn URLs and any answers you share.

Usage data: we use analytics tools (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) that collect information such as pages visited, clicks, device type, approximate location and how you move through the page. These tools use cookies or similar technology.

How we use it

To reply to you, write the free sample posts you request, prepare for and hold calls, send follow ups about our services, and improve this website. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the business, such as our form provider, Cal.com for scheduling, Google for email and calendar, and the analytics tools above. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep lead and client information for as long as it is useful for the relationship, and delete it on request.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you emails. Every email we send includes a way to opt out. Depending on your country (for example under GDPR in the EU and UK, or CCPA in California), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

International transfers

We work with founders in every country, so your information may be processed outside your own country, including in India and the United States.
